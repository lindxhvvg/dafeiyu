## What the license requires

CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 Section 2(a)(1) permits producing and sharing Adapted
Material for NonCommercial purposes only. Section 3(a) requires attribution,
an indication of changes, and a license notice. Section 3(b) requires an
Adapter's License with the same License Elements (BY, NC, SA), version 4.0 or
later, or a BY-NC-SA Compatible License, when Adapted Material is shared.
`3d/LICENSE.md` is that Adapter's License for the 3D files.
